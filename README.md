## Problem Description
The Dangote Cement dataset contains sales and operational information covering 500 transactions recorded between January and December 2023. The dataset includes information on sales revenue, quantity sold, products, customer types, regions, states, depots, payment methods, delivery status, production costs, profit margins, and sales representatives.

The main challenge was to analyze the available sales and operational data to understand business performance, identify important sales patterns, evaluate product and customer performance, monitor regional and depot activities, and assess profitability and delivery performance.

The analysis was carried out using Excel and Power BI to transform the raw data into meaningful business insights that can support decision-making and performance monitoring.
## Project Objectives
The objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales performance during 2023.
- Measure total sales revenue and quantity sold.
- Identify the highest-performing regions and states.
- Analyze sales performance by product type.
- Examine customer categories and their contribution to sales.
- Evaluate depot performance.
- Analyze payment methods used by customers.
- Monitor delivery status and identify potential operational issues.
- Compare production costs with sales revenue.
- Evaluate profitability and profit margins.
Identify monthly sales trends.
Develop an interactive dashboard for monitoring key business performance indicators.

## Key Findings
Overall Sales Performance

The dataset recorded 500 transactions with total sales revenue of approximately ₦73.62 million. Total quantity sold was approximately 26,482.10 tonnes, while the average sales value per transaction was approximately ₦147,237.76.

#### Regional Performance

The South South region recorded the highest total sales at approximately ₦14.16 million. Other regional sales included North West, North Central, North East, South East, and South West.

#### Product Performance

The dataset contained three major product categories:

- Bulk Cement – 188 transactions
- Bagged Cement – 163 transactions
- Clinker – 149 transactions

Bulk Cement recorded the highest sales revenue and the highest quantity sold, with approximately 9,909.29 tonnes.
#### Customer Performance
Three major customer categories were identified:

- Construction Companies
- Distributors
- Retailers

Construction Companies generated the highest total sales at approximately ₦26 million, followed by Distributors and Retailers.

#### Depot Performance

Yobe Depot recorded the highest sales among the depots, at approximately ₦6 million. Other strong-performing depots included Sokoto, Delta, Enugu, and Niger.

#### Payment Methods

The dataset recorded four payment methods: Credit, Cash, POS, and Bank Transfer. Credit was the most frequently recorded payment method, with 134 transactions.
#### Delivery Perfomance
Delivery status consisted of:

- Cancelled – 176 transactions
- Pending – 169 transactions
- Delivered – 148 transactions
- Unknown – 7 transactions

The relatively high number of cancelled and pending transactions represents an important operational issue requiring monitoring.

#### Profitability

Total production cost was approximately ₦53.50 million, while estimated gross profit was approximately ₦20.12 million. The average recorded profit margin was approximately 27.34%.

#### Monthly Performance

December recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately ₦11.56 million, while May recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately ₦2.96 million.
## Recommendation
- Improve delivery management by investigating the causes of cancelled and pending deliveries.
- Continue monitoring the South South region and identify factors contributing to its sales performance.
- Maintain adequate availability of Bulk Cement because of its strong sales and quantity performance.
- Strengthen relationships with Construction Companies, Distributors, and Retailers.
- Investigate the practices contributing to strong depot performance and assess whether they can be applied elsewhere.
- Regularly compare production costs with sales revenue to protect profit margins.
- Monitor credit transactions to manage outstanding receivables and support healthy cash flow.
- Use monthly sales dashboards to identify weaker sales periods and support performance improvement.
- Maintain an executive dashboard for continuous monitoring of sales, costs, products, customers, delivery status, and regional performance.
## Conclusion
The Dangote Cement Sales Analysis provides an overview of the company's sales and operational performance during 2023.

The analysis recorded 500 transactions, approximately ₦73.62 million in sales revenue, and approximately 26,482.10 tonnes of cement sold. South South recorded the highest regional sales, Bulk Cement was the leading product category, Construction Companies generated the highest customer-category sales, and Yobe recorded the highest depot-level sales.

The analysis also identified delivery performance as an important area for management attention because cancelled and pending transactions represented a substantial portion of the dataset.
